About

I studied molecular and cellular biology at Harvard, graduating cum laude, and spent time as a research fellow at Harvard Medical School. While there, I founded Checkmate Diabetes, a venture-backed startup applying machine learning and behavioral psychology to diabetes management, which won first place in Harvard's i3 Innovation Challenge.

From 2019 to 2022, I was an investment partner at Joyance Partners, an early-stage fund investing at the intersection of delight, behavior change, and human wellbeing. That vantage point on a lot of other people's companies is a large part of why I went back to building one of my own.
